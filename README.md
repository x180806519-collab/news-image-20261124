# news-image-20261124

One image summing up the world news of Tuesday 24 November 2026, drawn as a
newspaper front page.

## The image

`entry.png` (1024×1024, PNG) carries six stories:

1. **NVIDIA** — Jensen Huang is reinstated as CEO under pressure from Trump;
   shares jump 31% in a day, adding about $950 billion, and the S&P 500 rises 4.7%.
2. **USA** — Democrats secure at least 258 House seats, their largest majority
   since 1992, after Chuck Hubbard beats Virginia Foxx in North Carolina.
3. **LIBYA** — Ukrainian naval drones sink the Russian corvette Merkury at
   Benghazi; a Ka-27 helicopter is destroyed and the cargo ship Angara is hit.
4. **IRAN** — The IRGC says three tankers caught fire after hitting naval mines
   in the Strait of Hormuz despite a US escort; the claims are unverified.
5. **GAZA** — An Israeli airstrike in Gaza City kills Naeem Abu Nahel, director
   of Hamas' internal security, and six more people across Gaza.
6. **INDONESIA** — The death toll from the candy factory fire in Banten rises
   to 26, with eight workers still missing.

Sources: Reuters, Associated Press, CNN, IRNA.

## The program

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`. It runs offline and writes identical bytes
on every run.
